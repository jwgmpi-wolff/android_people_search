# Privacy and safety

## Data behavior

- Search text remains in app memory while the process runs.
- Search history is disabled by default.
- Enabling history stores at most 20 compact local entries in Preferences DataStore.
- Disabling history deletes saved history.
- **Clear local data** removes all settings and history.
- Android backup and device transfer exclude app preferences.
- There are no analytics, advertising IDs, cloud AI calls, hidden network calls, or background tracking.

Opening a result sends the encoded query to the selected public website in a browser Custom Tab. That website's privacy policy then applies.

## Acceptable use

Use the app only for lawful, respectful research. Do not use it for stalking, harassment, doxxing, credential or breach discovery, private-address discovery, deanonymization, discrimination, or unlawful surveillance. Results can be wrong or refer to another person. A confidence label is not proof of identity.

## Permissions

Only `android.permission.INTERNET` is declared. Contacts and device data are never searched.
